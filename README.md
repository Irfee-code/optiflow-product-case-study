# OptiFlow — Smart Patient Flow System

A product concept designed to reduce patient waiting time and improve coordination across high-volume eye hospitals.

OptiFlow focuses on identifying bottlenecks across registration, diagnostics, counselling, billing, pharmacy, and patient communication, and redesigning the journey around faster handoffs and better resource utilization.

## 🎯 Product Goal

Reduce non-clinical waiting time while allowing hospital staff to coordinate patient movement more efficiently.

The solution explores:

- AI-assisted registration and resource assignment
- Nurse-driven scan and payment workflows
- Real-time patient-flow coordination
- Digital payment integration
- Pharmacy pre-packing workflows
- AI-assisted patient education while waiting
- Faster identification of patients requiring dilation
